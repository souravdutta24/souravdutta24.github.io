# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Academic and research-community visitors: potential collaborators, conference or session organizers, PhD advisors' networks, hiring committees at universities and national labs, colleagues at the Oden Institute and ERDC, and researchers discovering the work via a citation or Google Scholar. Most arrive from a specific reference — a paper citation, a conference program, a direct referral — and scan quickly for current affiliation, research focus, and how to get in touch or find the CV/publications.

## Product Purpose

A professional homepage that establishes Sourav Dutta's research identity (reduced-order modeling, computational fluid dynamics, scientific machine learning) at a glance, keeps recent activity visibly current (news, publications), and gives visitors a fast path to the CV and contact info. Success is a visitor leaving with an accurate, up-to-date picture of the work and a clear way to follow up.

## Positioning

The single, current, authoritative record of Dutta's research identity and output — more complete and up-to-date than any one publication listing or social profile.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: view/download the CV or read a selected publication (primary); reach out by email or LinkedIn/GitHub/Scholar (secondary).
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "Sourav Dutta — researcher at the Oden Institute, working on ML-driven reduced-order models for environmental and coastal hydrodynamics."
- Belief ladder: this is real, current, credible research → the work is technically rigorous and published in respected venues → the person is active and reachable → worth citing, reading, or reaching out to.
- Proof on hand: 8 real publications with DOIs/arXiv links, named institutional affiliation (Oden Institute, UT Austin; previously ERDC), a downloadable CV.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous and authoritative. The design should read as substance-first — credentials and current work carry the page, not visual flourish. Confident and understated rather than flashy; the restraint itself signals seniority and rigor.

## Anti-references

No specific named anti-reference. Default guardrail: avoid AI-slop marketing-site tropes (gradient text, big hero CTAs, SaaS-style cards, glassmorphism) — this is a research homepage, not a product landing page.

## Design Principles

- Substance over spectacle: publications and current work are the hero; decoration never competes with them.
- Fast orientation: a visitor should grasp who this is and what they do within seconds of landing.
- Currency signals trust: news and recent activity should always look current, never stale.
- One canonical source: CV, publications, and contact info should never drift out of sync with each other.
- Restraint as authority: an uncluttered, minimal page reinforces rigor rather than undercutting it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA: sufficient color contrast in both light and dark mode, full keyboard navigation (including the search modal), alt text on all images, no motion-dependent content.
