Our paper "[Operator learning for predicting bulk wave parameters of spectral wave models](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.oceaneng.2026.127866)" has been published in Ocean Engineering. It explores the use of DeepONet to predict bulk wave parameters such as significant wave height and gradient of radiation stresses in both benchmark and realistic experiments, as obtained from steady state simulations using a spectral wave model SWAN.
