Our paper "[A neural operator emulator for coastal and riverine shallow water dynamics](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025JH000697)" has been published in the Journal of Geophysical Research:Machine Learning and Computation. It introduces MITONet, a neural operator emulator framework for modeling shallow water flows in realistic coastal and riverine environments.
